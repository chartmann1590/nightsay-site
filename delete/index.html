Delete your data

Nightsay: Snore & Sleep Talk · Developer: Hartmann Studios

Nightsay has no accounts. There is no sign-up, so there is no account to delete. Your recordings and night data (including any Wear OS heart rate and movement readings) are stored on your devices, and we never receive them. You delete them directly on your device.

Delete a clip or a night

  1. Open Nightsay and go to the Nights tab.
  2. Open a night. To remove one recording, tap Delete clip next to it. To remove the whole night and all its clips, tap Delete night.

Deleted clips and nights are removed from your phone immediately.

Delete everything

  • Clear the app's storage: Android Settings → Apps → Nightsay → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This deletes all recordings, night history and settings.
  • Or uninstall the app: uninstalling Nightsay deletes all of its recordings and data from your phone.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Recordings and night data: on your phone only, until you delete them or uninstall the app. On the free version, nights older than your last 3 are deleted automatically.
  • Wear OS heart rate and movement: stored on your devices only (and in your own Android backup if you turn it on). On the free version, readings older than your last 3 nights are deleted automatically; uninstalling Nightsay from your phone and watch removes all of them. Turn off sensor access in your watch's Settings → Apps → Nightsay → Permissions.
  • Android backup: if you have Android backup turned on, Android may hold a backup of the app's settings and night data (night summaries and any watch readings, never audio) in your own Google account. You can delete it in your Google account's backup settings or the Google Drive app (Backups).
  • Purchases: your Premium purchase record is kept by Google Play as part of your Google account. Cancel subscriptions in Google Play.
  • Advertising data: the free version's ads (Google AdMob) use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google retains ad data under its own policies.
  • Support emails: if you emailed us, we can delete that correspondence. Email me@charleshartmann.com with the subject "Nightsay data deletion" and we will delete it within 30 days.

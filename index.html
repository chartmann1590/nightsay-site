Questions

Do my recordings get uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, sound detection and storage all happen on your phone. A clip, reel, share card or backup file only leaves your phone if you choose to share or save it yourself, using Android's share menu or file picker.

Where does the AI run?

On your phone. Nightsay uses Google's Gemini Nano when your phone's Android system offers it. Otherwise, on phones with enough memory, you can download the open Qwen2.5 1.5B model once from Hugging Face; after that it runs offline. Only your night's numbers go into the model, never audio or transcripts. AI text can be wrong and is for wellness and fun, not medical advice.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Premium purchases are handled by Google Play and tied to your Google Play account.

Will it drain my battery?

Nightsay listens continuously, so we recommend keeping the phone plugged in overnight. The in-app battery guide helps with phones that stop background apps.

Can Nightsay tell me if I have a sleep problem?

No. Nightsay is for entertainment and wellness only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. If you are worried about your sleep or breathing, talk to a doctor.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a clip or a night inside the app, delete the AI model in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything on your phone. Details are on the data deletion page.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel Nightsay. See our Terms for details.

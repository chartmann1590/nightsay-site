Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · App: Nightsay: Snore & Sleep Talk (package com.charles.nightsay) · Developer: Hartmann Studios

This policy explains what information the Nightsay: Snore & Sleep Talk app ("Nightsay", "the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. Nightsay is published on Google Play by Hartmann Studios ("we", "us"). If you have questions, email me@charleshartmann.com.

The short version: Nightsay records audio with your microphone only while you are recording a night. Sound detection runs on your phone, and your recordings are stored only on your phone. We do not have servers that receive your audio, and we never see it. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. There is no account. If you use the optional Wear OS app and allow it, your watch's heart rate and movement readings are stored only on your devices too.

Microphone and your recordings

  • When the microphone is used: only after you tap Start, and only until you tap Stop. While recording, Android shows a persistent notification and the microphone indicator. The app asks for the microphone permission before the first recording; you can revoke it at any time in Android Settings.
  • What is kept: the app listens continuously while recording, but keeps only short audio clips around detected sounds (such as snoring, talking, coughing or loud noises). The rest of the audio is discarded and is never written to storage.
  • On-device processing: sounds are classified by Google's YAMNet audio model, which runs entirely on your phone through Google's MediaPipe library. No audio is sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else for analysis.
  • On-device storage: clips and night summaries (start and end times, detected sound types, timestamps, confidence levels and your snore score) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Audio clips are excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers. If Android backup is turned on, Android may back up the app's settings and night summaries (not audio) to your own Google account, under Google's control; you can turn this off in Android Settings.
  • Sharing is up to you: a clip leaves your phone only if you use the Share clip feature yourself (Premium) and choose an app to send it with. Whatever you share is then handled by the app or person you shared it with.
  • Other people in the room: the app can pick up the voices of anyone near your phone. Please let people who sleep near you know you are recording.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob on the results and history screens. Ads are never shown while recording. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device model, operating system and app information;
  • ad interactions (for example, ad views and taps) and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. AdMob never receives your recordings, night data or watch sensor data.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices: Settings → Google → Ads). You can also manage ad personalization at My Ad Center. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later; clearing the app's storage also resets it so the consent message appears again. Buying Premium removes ads.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment; we never receive your card number or billing details. Google provides the app with the purchase status (such as which plan is active and a purchase token) so the app can unlock Premium on your device. Google's handling of payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Wear OS companion app

The optional Nightsay app for Wear OS lets you start and stop recording from your watch and shows live status. It does not record audio.

  • Optional sensor data: if you allow it, the watch app reads your heart rate and movement (from the watch's motion sensor) during a recorded night. Android asks for your permission first, and the watch app works as a remote control without it.
  • Why: to show your overnight heart rate and movement next to that night's sounds in the morning report. This is for wellness and entertainment only and is not medical information.
  • Where it goes: the readings sync from your watch directly to the Nightsay app on your paired phone over the Wear OS data connection between your devices (Google's Wearable Data Layer). They are stored only on your devices, with the night they belong to. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, never shared with advertisers, and never used for ads.
  • Deleting it: deleting a night in the phone app deletes its heart rate and movement readings too. Uninstalling the apps removes all of it. You can stop sensor access at any time by turning off the permission on your watch (Settings → Apps → Nightsay → Permissions).

What we do not collect

  • We do not receive, store or listen to your audio recordings, and we never receive heart rate or movement data from the Wear OS app.
  • We do not require or create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location through the app.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and we use it only to reply to you. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

Security

Your recordings stay in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication between the app and Google's ad and billing services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

Data retention and deletion

  • On the free version, the app keeps your last 3 nights and automatically deletes older nights and their clips. With Premium, nights are kept until you delete them.
  • You can delete any single clip or an entire night inside the app at any time. Deleting removes it from your phone immediately.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Nightsay → Storage → Clear storage, deletes all of your recordings and night data.
  • Because we never receive your recordings, there is nothing on our side to delete. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained by Google under its own policies.

More detail is on our data deletion page.

Children

Nightsay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information (for example, by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in Europe or state privacy laws in the United States such as California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit certain processing. Most data the app uses never leaves your phone and is under your control there. For anything else, email me@charleshartmann.com and we will respond within 30 days. For data held by Google, use the tools linked above.

Not a medical device

Nightsay is for entertainment and wellness only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition.

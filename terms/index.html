1. The app

Nightsay records audio overnight on your Android device, detects sounds such as snoring, talking and coughing using an on-device model, keeps short clips of those sounds, and shows a morning report. Sound detection can be wrong: it may miss sounds or label them incorrectly, and recording can be interrupted by your device's battery or system settings.
